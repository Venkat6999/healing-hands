Physiotherapy care built around the patient in front of us

Healing Hands is a small, focused physiotherapy clinic in Hanamkonda — every session is led directly by one of our two physiotherapists.

Our story

A neighbourhood clinic, run by physiotherapists

Healing Hands Advanced Physiotherapy Clinic was set up to give patients in Hanamkonda direct access to experienced physiotherapists — without long waits or being passed between different staff for each visit.

From musculoskeletal pain and sports injuries to post-surgical rehabilitation, our treatment rooms are equipped for hands-on assessment, electrotherapy, manual therapy and structured exercise-based rehab.

  • ✓ Every session led by a qualified physiotherapist, not a technician
  • ✓ Dedicated treatment rooms for electrotherapy, manual therapy and exercise
  • ✓ Transparent, published pricing for every treatment

Inside the clinic
