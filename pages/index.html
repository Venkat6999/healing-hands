FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Some commonly asked questions about our physiotherapy services.

What types of conditions do you treat?

We treat orthopaedic, neurological, sports, paediatric, and geriatric conditions — including back pain, neck pain, knee injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, stroke recovery, cerebral palsy, and more.

Are your physiotherapists certified?

Yes. All our physiotherapists are highly qualified, certified in specialized techniques like Dry Needling, Manual Therapy, and Kinesio Taping, and have years of clinical experience.

Do you offer home physiotherapy?

Yes. We provide professional physiotherapy at home with expert oversight and strong quality checks. Contact us to schedule a home visit.

How do I book an appointment?

You can easily book an appointment by calling us, sending a WhatsApp message, or filling out our online booking form. Our team will assist you in scheduling your session.

How much does a session cost?

Basic electrotherapy sessions start at Rs. 400/-. Manual therapy is Rs. 500/-, Cupping therapy is Rs. 600/- and Dry needle therapy is Rs. 800/-.

Which physiotherapist should I see?

Dr. U. Kalyani generally sees patients earlier in the day, while Dr. A. Prakash is available in the evenings. Our reception team can match you to the right physiotherapist and time based on your condition.

What are your clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM – 8:30 PM. Sunday: 3:00 PM – 8:30 PM.
