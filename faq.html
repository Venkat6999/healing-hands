Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions patients ask us most often. Can't find yours? Call the clinic directly.

Call the clinic directly or message us on WhatsApp with your preferred day and time. Our reception team will confirm your slot and let you know which physiotherapist is available.
No referral is required. You can walk in or call to book directly, and our physiotherapist will carry out an initial assessment before recommending treatment.
Your first session starts with a consultation and physical assessment to understand your condition. Based on this, the physiotherapist will explain a suggested treatment plan and begin initial treatment if appropriate.
Pricing depends on the treatment required — basic electrotherapy sessions start at ₹300, with manual therapy, cupping and dry needling priced separately. See our Services & Pricing page for the full list.
Dr. U. Kalyani (PT) generally sees patients earlier in the day, while Dr. A. Prakash (PT), a specialist in Dry Needling and Cupping Therapy, is available in the evenings. Our reception team can help match you to the right physiotherapist and time based on your condition.
This varies by condition. Your physiotherapist will give you an estimated number of sessions after your first assessment, and will review progress with you regularly.
For patients who are unable to travel, please call the clinic to check availability for select home-visit treatments.
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 8:30 PM. We recommend calling ahead to confirm availability before you visit.
