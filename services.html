Our services

Services offered

Specialised physiotherapy services delivered by qualified, experienced physiotherapists.

Neuro Physiotherapy — Rehab

Stroke recovery, spinal cord injury rehab, cerebral palsy management and neurological condition support.

Sports Physiotherapy

Sports injury treatment, ligament rehab, return-to-play programmes and athletic performance support.

Paediatric Physiotherapy

Developmental delays, cerebral palsy, torticollis, clubfoot and childhood musculoskeletal conditions.

Geriatric Physiotherapy

Balance training, fall prevention, joint pain management and mobility support for older adults.

Home Care Physiotherapy

In-home physiotherapy sessions for patients who are unable to travel to the clinic.

Chiropractor Treatment

Spinal manipulation, joint mobilisation and musculoskeletal alignment for pain relief.

Modalities available

Therapies offered at the clinic

A comprehensive range of physiotherapy modalities and advanced treatment techniques.

⚡

Interferential Therapy (IFT)

One of the types of electrotherapy used for the management of pain, muscle spasms and inflammation through deep tissue stimulation.

🦴

Chiropractic Therapy

A hands-on technique focused on the diagnosis and treatment of neuromuscular disorders through spinal manipulation and joint mobilisation.

🔊

Ultrasound Therapy

Application of ultrasound waves above the range of human hearing to treat various types of injuries and promote tissue healing.

💡

Laser Therapy

Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation — low-level laser applied to reduce pain, inflammation and accelerate tissue repair.

🫙

Cupping Therapy

An ancient healing practice with a strong place in modern physiotherapy — used for pain relief, muscle recovery and improving blood flow.

🕯️

Wax Therapy

A form of deep heat therapy — one of the most effective ways of applying heat to improve mobility by warming joints and soft tissues.

🏷️

Kinesio Taping / Taping Therapy

Elastic therapeutic tape used as a rehabilitative method to facilitate natural healing while providing support and stability to muscles.

📍

Dry Needling Therapy

Also known as Trigger Point Dry Needling — a procedure using thin needles to release myofascial trigger points and relieve muscle pain.

🔥

Thermotherapy (Heat Therapy)

Application of heat through hot packs — the most common heating agent used in rehabilitation to reduce pain and stiffness.

⚡

TENS Therapy

Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation — electrical stimulation whose primary aim is to provide symptomatic pain relief.

💧

Lymphatic Drainage Massage

A gentle massage technique that stimulates the lymphatic system to help the body eliminate waste, toxins and excess fluid.

🏋️

Overhead Track Harness Therapy

Physical therapy using a ceiling-mounted track system and harness to support body weight during gait training and rehabilitation.

🔗

Traction Therapy

Gentle pulling force applied to the spine to relieve pressure on discs and nerves, reducing pain and improving mobility.

⚙️

Spinal Decompression

Advanced traction therapy that gently stretches the spine to relieve pressure on herniated or bulging discs.

🌡️

Tecar / CRET Therapy

Capacitive and Resistive Electrical Transfer — radiofrequency energy that penetrates deep tissue to accelerate natural healing.

❄️

Cryotherapy (Cold Therapy)

Application of cold to reduce inflammation, swelling and pain — commonly used for acute injuries and post-surgical recovery.

💨

Dynamic Compression Therapy

Intermittent pneumatic compression to improve blood circulation, reduce swelling and prevent blood clots in limbs.

💥

Shockwave Therapy

High-energy acoustic waves applied to affected areas to stimulate healing in chronic musculoskeletal conditions.

🤖

Robotic Spinal Decompression

Computer-controlled robotic system for precise, targeted spinal decompression therapy with real-time feedback.

🔥

Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)

Minimally invasive procedure using radio waves to heat and destroy nerve tissue, providing long-term pain relief.

🩸

PRP Injections

Platelet-Rich Plasma injections — concentrated blood platelets injected into injured tissue to promote natural healing.

💉

Facet Joint Injections

Targeted injections into the facet joints to diagnose and treat pain originating from spinal arthritis or injury.

💉

Epidural Steroid Injection (ESI)

Corticosteroid medication injected into the epidural space to reduce inflammation around spinal nerves.

💉

Nerve Block Injection

Anaesthetic or anti-inflammatory injection near a nerve to block pain signals from a specific area of the body.

💉

Trigger Point Injection

Medication injected directly into myofascial trigger points to relieve chronic muscle pain and tension.

🔊

Ultrasound-Guided Joint Injection

Precise injection into a joint using real-time ultrasound imaging for accurate needle placement and better outcomes.

🤲

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques including joint mobilisation, manipulation and soft tissue work to restore movement and reduce pain.

🫳

Myofascial Release (MFR)

Sustained pressure applied to the myofascial connective tissue to eliminate restrictions and restore motion.

🫳

Soft Tissue Mobilization

Direct techniques applied to muscles, tendons and ligaments to improve tissue mobility and reduce pain.

🧍

Pelvic Floor Physical Therapy

Specialised exercises and manual therapy to strengthen pelvic floor muscles and treat incontinence or pelvic pain.

🫁

Chest Physiotherapy

Techniques to clear mucus from the lungs and improve breathing — percussion, postural drainage and breathing exercises.

🌡️

Shortwave Diathermy (SWD)

High-frequency electromagnetic energy applied to deep tissues to generate heat, reducing pain and promoting healing.
